DOCUMENT_ID: POLICY-2025-002
TITLE: Enterprise Remote Work Policy (2025 Revised)
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2025
STATUS: Active / Current (Supersedes POLICY-2024-001)

1. Hybrid & Remote Work Framework
Effective January 1, 2025, this policy replaces all prior remote work guidelines (including POLICY-2024-001):
- Tier 1 Roles (Engineering, Product, Architecture): Fully remote allowed without special manager sign-off.
- Tier 2 Roles (Operations, Sales, HR): Up to three (3) days remote per week.
- Tier 3 Roles (On-site Facilities, Datacenter Ops): 100% on-site required.

2. Home Office Allowance Update
The home office stipend has been increased to $750 USD per calendar year (renewable annually). Purchases must be routed directly through the IT Procurement Portal rather than submitted as manual expense reports.
