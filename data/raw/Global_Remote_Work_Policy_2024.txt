TITLE: Enterprise Remote Work Policy (2024 Edition)
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2024
STATUS: Legacy / Partial Invalidation

1. Workplace Flexibility
Employees across all departments are eligible to work remotely for up to two (2) days per week, subject to direct manager approval. Full-time remote work is not permitted under standard contracts.

2. Equipment & Home Office Expense
The company provides a one-time home office equipment stipend of $500 USD upon hire. Employees must submit receipts through the standard expense portal within 30 days of purchase.
